How it works

From first call to flight home.

You make three decisions: what, where and how long. We take care of everything in between.

01Conversation

A 30-minute call with a care advisor about your goals and health history, or begin with our concierge online.

02Medical intake

A short, secure questionnaire. Our physicians tailor your screening and flag anything to prepare in advance.

03Your plan

A fixed, all-inclusive quote and a day-by-day itinerary. Your visa invitation letter is issued within 48 hours.

04The journey

Your coordinator meets you on arrival. Hospital, resort and villa are pre-briefed; transfers between legs are escorted.

05Aftercare

A 90-day follow-up with your physician, and your records shared securely with your doctor at home.
